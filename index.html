    Survival, one choice at a time.

    Explore the streets, gather supplies, and find a safe route out. A flashlight and ammo will help you make it through the night—but clear any danger in your way before you escape.

    • Explore to discover routes, supplies, and encounters.
    • Rest to recover up to 15 health. A medkit restores up to 30 when you choose to use it.
    • Fight to wear down a zombie, or run at a cost of 10 health.
    • Use ammo during an encounter to get away. Checking it in your bag does not consume it.
    • Use a mouse, touch, or Tab and Enter to choose. There is no timer.

    Your progress stays in memory only. Refreshing the page or starting a new story resets it.
